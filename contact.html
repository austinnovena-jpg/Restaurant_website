FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to things our customers often ask

How do I place an order?

Simply browse our menu, select your favorite dishes, add them to your cart, and proceed to checkout. It's that easy! You can also call us directly to place your order.

How long does delivery take?

We aim to deliver within 30-45 minutes. During peak hours, it might take up to an hour. We always prioritize getting your food to you hot and fresh!

Do you have vegetarian/vegan options?

Absolutely! We have a dedicated section for vegetarian and vegan dishes. We also have gluten-free options available. Just check our menu for items marked with special tags.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and cash on delivery.

Do you offer catering for events?

Yes! We offer catering services for corporate events, private parties, weddings, and more. Contact us directly to discuss your event needs and get a custom quote.

Can I customize my order?

Of course! We love making things just the way you like them. Just leave a note in the special instructions when ordering, or call us directly with your preferences.
